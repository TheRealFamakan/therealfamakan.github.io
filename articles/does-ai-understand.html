Does an intelligent system understand?

Published September 1, 2026 · 6 min read

A language model can produce an answer that feels thoughtful. That ability raises a deceptively simple question: is producing meaningful language the same as understanding?

Meaning is more than sequence

A model learns patterns between words and contexts. These patterns can be remarkably useful, but usefulness alone does not settle the question of whether there is an inner point of view behind the response.

Understanding has consequences

Human understanding is connected to memory, action, embodiment, and responsibility. We do not only describe a situation; we are affected by it and act within it. This difference matters when we decide what to trust.

A useful distinction

It may be more productive to distinguish functional understanding from lived understanding. An AI system can manipulate concepts well enough to help us reason, while still leaving the philosophical question of experience open.

The important point is not to dismiss the technology or to grant it a human status too quickly. It is to keep asking precise questions about what the system does and what we mean by intelligence.
